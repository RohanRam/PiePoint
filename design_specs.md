# PiePoint Design Specs

## 🎨 Color Palette
*   **Primary Brand:** `#FF6B35` (Orange Accent) | `#FF8C5E` (Orange Light)
*   **Secondary/Rating:** `#FFFFC107` (Yellow Accent)
*   **Backgrounds:** `#FAFAFA` (App Bg) | `#FFFFFF` (Cards/Surfaces) | `#F5F5F5` (Inner Cards)
*   **Typography:** `#1A1A2E` (Primary Navy) | `#6B7280` (Secondary Slate)

## ✍️ Typography
*   **Headings/Titles:** Bold or ExtraBold (`22sp` to `57sp`)
*   **Body & Labels:** Normal or Medium (`11sp` to `16sp`)

## 🧱 Design Consistency
*   **Corner Radii:** Cards (`24dp`) | Buttons (`20dp`) | Chips/Selectors (`50dp` pill)
*   **Tactile Feedback:** Scales down to `0.96x` on press with spring animation; shadow drops from `8dp` to `2dp`.
*   **Visual Elements:** Glassmorphism accents (white with alpha `0.3` to `0.6`) and subtle borders (`#E0E0E0`).

## ⚖️ Layout & Balance
*   **Grid:** Strict alignment using an `8dp` spacing system.
*   **Contrast:** Heavy title weights coupled with muted body colors for high scannability.
